---

## 🧠 Theory

**Privacy-Enhancing Technologies (PETs)** are technologies designed to reduce unnecessary exposure or misuse of personal information while allowing useful digital activities to continue.

Examples include:

- VPNs
- End-to-end encrypted messaging
- Encryption
- Anonymous or privacy-preserving credentials
- Differential privacy
- Privacy-preserving computation

---
