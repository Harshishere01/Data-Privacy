# ⚖️ Practical 03 — Regulation Compliance

> **Subject:** Data Privacy  
> **Practical No.:** 03  
> **Topic:** Data Protection Regulation Compliance

---

## 🎯 Aim

To study important data protection requirements and prepare a basic compliance plan for an organization that processes personal data.

---

## 📚 Objectives

- Understand the purpose of data protection regulations.
- Identify important privacy obligations.
- Map organizational activities to compliance requirements.
- Develop a basic compliance checklist.

---

## 🧠 Theory

Data protection regulations establish requirements for organizations that collect and process personal information.

Common regulatory concepts include:

- Lawful and transparent processing
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Security
- Accountability
- Individual rights

The exact requirements depend on the applicable jurisdiction, organization, and type of processing.

---

## 📋 Compliance Checklist

| Requirement | Organization Action | Status |
|---|---|:---:|
| Data inventory | Maintain a record of personal data | ✅ |
| Purpose specification | Document purposes of processing | ✅ |
| Data minimization | Review unnecessary fields | ⚠️ |
| Access controls | Implement role-based access | ⚠️ |
| Security measures | Encryption and authentication | ⚠️ |
| Retention | Define retention periods | ⚠️ |
| Privacy notices | Inform individuals appropriately | ✅ |
| Data subject requests | Establish request-handling procedure | ⚠️ |
| Breach response | Maintain incident response procedure | ⚠️ |
| Third-party processors | Review vendors and contracts | ⚠️ |

---

## 🛠️ Compliance Plan

### Step 1 — Identify Data

Create an inventory of all personal data collected and processed.

### Step 2 — Identify Processing Purposes

Document why each category of information is collected and used.

### Step 3 — Review Access

Ensure employees can access only information required for their responsibilities.

### Step 4 — Apply Security Controls

Use appropriate authentication, encryption, monitoring, and access controls.

### Step 5 — Establish Retention Rules

Define how long information is retained and when it should be deleted or anonymized.

### Step 6 — Handle Individual Rights

Create procedures for responding to applicable requests from individuals.

### Step 7 — Prepare for Incidents

Maintain a documented process for identifying, containing, investigating, and responding to data breaches.

---

## 📝 Result

A basic data protection compliance plan was prepared by identifying important privacy requirements and mapping them to organizational controls.

## 🎤 Viva Questions

**Q1. What is data protection compliance?**  
It is the process of ensuring that an organization's data processing practices follow applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

**Q2. What is purpose limitation?**  
It means personal data should be collected and used for specified purposes.

**Q3. What is accountability?**  
Accountability means an organization should be able to demonstrate that it follows applicable privacy requirements.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

A structured compliance program helps organizations manage personal data responsibly and demonstrate adherence to applicable data protection requirements.
