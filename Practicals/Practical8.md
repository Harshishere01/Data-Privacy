## 🎯 Aim

To develop a privacy breach response plan describing the actions an organization should take when personal data is compromised.

---

## 📚 Objectives

- Understand the concept of a data breach.
- Identify stages of incident response.
- Define roles and responsibilities.
- Develop a communication strategy.
- Understand the importance of documentation and post-incident review.

---

## 🧠 Theory

A **data breach** is an incident in which personal or confidential information is accessed, disclosed, altered, lost, or otherwise compromised without authorization.

An effective response plan helps an organization respond systematically and reduce further harm.

---

## 🚨 Example Scenario

**Organization:** ABC Technologies  
**Incident:** Unauthorized access to a customer database  
**Potentially affected information:** Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and order information.
