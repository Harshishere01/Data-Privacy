# 📊 Practical 02 — Privacy Impact Assessment

> **Subject:** Data Privacy  
> **Practical No.:** 02  
> **Topic:** Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)

---

## 🎯 Aim

To conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) of a proposed technology system and identify potential privacy risks and suitable mitigation measures.

---

## 📚 Objectives

- Understand the concept of a Privacy Impact Assessment.
- Identify personal data processed by a system.
- Analyze potential privacy risks.
- Determine suitable privacy controls.
- Apply privacy-by-design principles.

---

## 🧠 Theory

A **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** is a structured process used to identify and assess privacy risks associated with a project, technology, or information system.

A PIA generally considers:

1. What data is collected?
2. Why is it collected?
3. How is it used?
4. Who can access it?
5. How long is it retained?
6. What controls protect it?

---

## 💻 Case Study

### Smart Campus Attendance System

A university plans to introduce a digital attendance system using student identification and attendance records.

### Data Collected

| Data | Purpose | Sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| Student Name | Identification | Medium |
| Student ID | Attendance identification | Medium |
| Attendance Records | Attendance management | Medium |
| Login Credentials | Authentication | High |
| Device Information | System security | Medium |

---

## 🔎 Privacy Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Risk |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| Unauthorized access | 3 | 3 | 🔴 High |
| Excessive data collection | 2 | 2 | 🟡 Medium |
| Excessive retention | 2 | 2 | 🟡 Medium |
| Data leakage | 2 | 3 | 🔴 High |
| Unauthorized sharing | 2 | 3 | 🔴 High |

---

## 🛡️ Mitigation Measures

- Collect only necessary information.
- Apply strong authentication.
- Restrict access according to roles.
- Encrypt sensitive information.
- Define a clear retention period.
- Maintain audit logs.
- Conduct periodic privacy reviews.
- Provide appropriate information to students about data processing.

---

## 🔐 Privacy-by-Design Principles

The system should incorporate privacy controls from the beginning rather than adding them after deployment.

Examples include:

- Data minimization
- Secure defaults
- Access control
- Encryption
- Transparency
- Limited retention
- User rights management

---

## 📝 Result

A Privacy Impact Assessment was conducted for the Smart Campus Attendance System. Major privacy risks were identified and appropriate controls were proposed.

---

## 🎤 Viva Questions

**Q1. What is a PIA?**  
A PIA is a structured assessment used to identify and manage privacy risks associated with a project or system.

**Q2. Why is a PIA performed?**  
It helps identify privacy risks before or during the development of a system.

**Q3. What is Privacy by Design?**  
It means incorporating privacy considerations into the design and development of a system from the beginning.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

A PIA helps organizations identify privacy risks early and design systems that handle personal data responsibly.
